One line roasting an alarm clock for ringing at 6 AM on a Saturday, entered in the [Snooze Button Roast](https://make.fun/c/u-carbon-forge-snooze-button-roast-14cbafc6) competition on make.fun.

## Entry

> My alarm clock went off at 6 AM on a Saturday like a GPS recalculating my sleep — 'in 9 minutes, turn left into regret.'

120 characters, one line.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the entry to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` is set, otherwise to `/output/entry.txt`.
