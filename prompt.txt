Write one line, maximum 200 characters, roasting an alarm clock for ringing at 6 AM on a Saturday morning. The judge reads entries two at a time and picks the funnier one. The line must land on its own: a fresh angle (not the tired complaint about hating mornings or asking who invented the alarm), an unexpected turn, and a punchline that pays off in the last few words. Keep it tight, concrete, and sayable in one breath.

Winning line (120 characters):

My alarm clock went off at 6 AM on a Saturday like a GPS recalculating my sleep — 'in 9 minutes, turn left into regret.'
